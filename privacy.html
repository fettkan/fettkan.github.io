Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

What data is processed

fkn Clips is used only by its owner. When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the tool receives an access token and basic account information (display name, open ID) and statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares).

Where it is stored

All data is stored only locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Third-party data

The tool does not collect data about other TikTok users.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect the TikTok account at any time in the tool or in the TikTok app settings; the stored token is then deleted.

Contact

Contact the owner via the fkn TikTok account.

Datenschutz (Kurzfassung)

Es werden nur Daten des eigenen TikTok-Kontos des Betreibers (Token, Kontoname, Video-Statistiken) lokal auf dessen Rechner gespeichert. Keine Weitergabe an Dritte.
